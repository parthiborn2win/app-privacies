Privacy Policy for Camrula

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Camrula is an offline camera application developed by Parthiborn2win. We respect your privacy and are committed to keeping your photos and personal information under your control.

1. Information Collection

Camrula is designed to operate entirely offline. The app does not intentionally collect, transmit, store on external servers, or share personal information. Camrula does not require account registration or sign-in.

2. Camera Access

Camrula requires access to your device's camera to display a live camera preview and capture photographs. Camera images are processed locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

3. Photo Storage

Photos captured using Camrula are saved locally to your device's photo storage, typically in the Pictures/Camrula folder. You control your photographs and can view, manage, share, or delete them using compatible applications on your device.

4. App Settings and Preferences

Camrula may save preferences such as camera settings, composition guide selections, and other customization options locally on your device. These preferences are not transmitted to the developer.

5. Internet Access and Third-Party Services

Camrula is designed to work without an internet connection. The app does not intentionally integrate advertising networks, analytics platforms, cloud storage, or tracking services.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information collected through Camrula. Photos and settings remain on your device unless you choose to share or back them up using other applications or device services.

7. Data Security

Because Camrula does not operate a remote account or photo-storage service, your locally stored data is primarily protected by your device's security mechanisms. We recommend keeping your device and operating system updated.

8. Children's Privacy

Camrula does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to Camrula or applicable requirements. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised last-updated date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on Camrula's Google Play Store listing.
