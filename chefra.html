Chefra Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

1. About Chefra

Chefra (package name: com.parthiborn2win.chefra) is a cooking and recipe management application for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Chefra.

2. Information stored on your device

Chefra stores recipes you create, recipe details, selected photos or document references, local profile details and preferences, favourites, cooking history, timers and app settings on your device. These features do not currently upload your local recipe collection or profile to a Chefra-operated cloud account.

Chefra does not currently offer account registration or sign-in. A local profile is not an online account.

3. Public recipes and Supabase

When you access public recipes, Chefra makes encrypted HTTPS requests to a Supabase-hosted catalogue and image storage service to retrieve recipe information and images. Search terms entered into the public recipe search are processed on your device and are not intentionally included in catalogue search requests.

As with other online services, Supabase and its infrastructure providers may process technical request information such as IP address, request URL or resource path, date and time, HTTP status, and device or software information in request headers. Such information may be used to deliver the service, maintain reliability and security, investigate errors, or comply with legal obligations. Actual server-side logging, retention and any location inference depend on the provider and its configuration. Chefra does not intentionally send its locally generated installation or profile UUIDs with these catalogue requests.

4. Voice guidance and text-to-speech

Chefra offers spoken cooking instructions and reminders using the text-to-speech engine configured on your device. Voice guidance may be enabled by default, and you can turn it off in app settings or mute it during cooking.

The text provided for speech can include recipe instructions, ingredients and user-entered cooking or reminder text. Depending on your device, selected voice and text-to-speech provider, that text may be processed online by the provider. The provider’s privacy practices, retention and security controls are governed by its own policies. Chefra does not control which third-party text-to-speech engine is installed or whether that engine uses online processing.

5. Microphone and whistle detection

If you enable automatic pressure-cooker whistle detection and grant microphone permission, Chefra uses microphone audio while the relevant feature is active to detect whistles. Audio samples are processed locally in memory. The inspected implementation does not save or upload microphone recordings. You can stop detection and manage microphone permission through your device settings.

6. Photos and files

You may choose a photo or document from your device or a connected document provider for use with a local recipe or profile. Chefra stores references to selected items and displays them locally; the current implementation does not upload the selected photo or document bytes to Chefra servers. If you select content from a cloud-backed document provider, that provider’s own policies may apply.

7. Sharing recipes

When you choose Share, Chefra sends the selected recipe text to the app or service you choose using Android’s sharing interface. The recipient may handle the information according to its own privacy policy. Sharing is initiated by you.

8. Backup and device transfer

Chefra supports Android-managed backup and device transfer of selected saved information, which may include saved recipes, completed cooking history, favourites, preferences and local profile details. These backups are managed by your device and backup provider, not by a Chefra-operated recipe-sync account.

Backup availability, encryption, retention and deletion depend on your Android version, device and backup provider. Previous backups may remain after you reset local Chefra data. You can manage backup settings through your device or backup provider.

9. Permissions

Internet access is used to retrieve public recipes and images. Microphone access is used for optional whistle detection. Notification, alarm, wake-lock and restart-related capabilities support cooking timers, reminders and related app functions. Chefra does not require location, contacts or SMS permissions for its inspected features.

10. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you edit or delete it, reset Chefra’s local data, uninstall the app, or it is affected by device or backup behavior. Chefra provides a Reset local Chefra data function. Resetting local data does not erase copies previously shared with another app, files held by an external provider, previously created Android backups, or records that may be retained by network or text-to-speech providers.

Chefra does not currently provide online account deletion because it does not offer online accounts. For privacy questions, contact [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL]. We cannot promise deletion of provider-managed logs or backups that we do not control.

11. Security

Chefra uses HTTPS for its app-controlled public catalogue and recipe-image requests and stores local application data in Android app storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Online text-to-speech processing and Android backup security depend on the relevant external provider and device configuration.

12. Children’s privacy

Chefra is a general cooking utility and is not specifically designed for children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to an external service through Chefra, contact us at [INSERT CONTACT EMAIL] so we can review the concern and explain any steps within our control.

13. Third-party services

Chefra uses Supabase infrastructure for public recipe catalogue delivery, Android system services for device backup and sharing, and the device-selected text-to-speech provider for voice guidance. The applicable third-party services may process information under their own terms and privacy policies.
